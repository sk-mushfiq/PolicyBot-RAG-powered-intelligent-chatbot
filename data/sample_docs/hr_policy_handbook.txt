SECTION 1: LEAVE POLICY
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

1.1 Annual Leave
All full-time employees are entitled to 20 working days of annual leave
per calendar year. Part-time employees receive leave on a pro-rata basis.
Leave entitlement accrues from the first day of employment.

Unused leave may be carried forward up to a maximum of 5 days into the
following calendar year. Any unused leave beyond this limit will be
forfeited unless special approval is granted by HR.

1.2 Sick Leave
Employees are entitled to 10 days of paid sick leave per year. For
absences exceeding 3 consecutive days, a medical certificate from a
registered doctor is required. Sick leave cannot be carried forward and
does not accumulate.

1.3 Casual Leave
Each employee receives 5 days of casual leave per year for personal
emergencies. Casual leave must be applied at least 24 hours in advance
except in cases of genuine emergency. Approval is at the discretion of
the line manager.

1.4 Leave Application Process
All leave requests must be submitted through the HR portal at least
5 working days in advance for annual leave, and 1 working day in advance
for casual leave. Emergency leave should be communicated to the line
manager via phone or email immediately, followed by a formal application
within 2 working days of return.

SECTION 2: CODE OF CONDUCT
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

2.1 Professional Conduct
All employees are expected to conduct themselves professionally and
respectfully at all times. This includes interactions with colleagues,
clients, vendors, and any other business associates.

2.2 Harassment Policy
SMR maintains a zero-tolerance policy toward any form of harassment,
including verbal, physical, or digital harassment. Any incident must be
reported to the HR department within 5 working days. Investigations will
be completed within 30 days of the report.

2.3 Confidentiality
Employees must maintain strict confidentiality regarding all proprietary
business information, client data, and internal communications. This
obligation continues for a period of 2 years after the termination of
employment.

SECTION 3: ONBOARDING POLICY
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

3.1 Probation Period
All new employees undergo a 3-month probation period. During this period,
performance will be reviewed monthly. Confirmation of employment is
subject to satisfactory completion of the probation period.

3.2 Training & Onboarding
New employees must complete the mandatory onboarding programme within
the first 2 weeks of joining. This includes compliance training, tool
setup, team introductions, and a departmental orientation session.

3.3 Equipment & Access
IT equipment will be provided on the first day of joining. Software
access and credentials will be provisioned within 24 hours of joining.
Employees are responsible for the safekeeping of all company-issued
equipment.

SECTION 4: REMOTE WORK POLICY
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

4.1 Eligibility
Employees who have completed their probation period are eligible to apply
for remote work arrangements. Remote work is subject to manager approval
and departmental requirements.

4.2 Work Hours
Remote employees must be available and responsive during core business
hours of 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM local time. Attendance at scheduled meetings
is mandatory regardless of work location.

4.3 Security
Remote employees must use VPN when accessing company systems. Use of
public Wi-Fi for company work is strictly prohibited without VPN.
